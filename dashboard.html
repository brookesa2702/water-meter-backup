Latest readings are cumulative meter totals. Consumption is the difference between consecutive readings. These CSV timestamps include polling times and any recovered device report times. The chart assigns each short interval to its ending timestamp; this is an approximation, especially across period boundaries. Intervals longer than 90 minutes, meter resets and invalid rows are excluded from consumption totals rather than estimated. A dash means no qualifying interval. Quarter means January–March, April–June, July–September or October–December. Local timestamps contain no UTC offset, so the repeated hour at the autumn clock change cannot be reliably distinguished.
