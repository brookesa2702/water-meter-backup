All three consumption series use the same overlapping time intervals. House only = Total property − Garden; Garden is already included in Total property. Because reporting times differ, cumulative readings are linearly interpolated between samples no more than 90 minutes apart; this estimates timing and does not create exact hourly measurements. Intervals are split at hour boundaries before daily or calendar-quarter aggregation. No extrapolation is used. Gaps, meter resets, duplicate local timestamps and intervals where Garden exceeds Total property are excluded from all three series. Unknown consumption is not counted as zero. Cumulative meter totals may have different starting offsets, so they are never directly subtracted. Quarter means calendar quarter. Timestamps are Cyprus local time without a UTC offset; the repeated autumn hour cannot be resolved reliably.
